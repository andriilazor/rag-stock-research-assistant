# Nordwind shares slide after gearbox failures shut turbines at Skarvhavet

**Bergen, 8 October 2026** — Shares of Nordwind Energy fell sharply on Thursday after the company said it had taken 14 turbines offline at its flagship Skarvhavet offshore wind farm following the discovery of gearbox defects.

In a stock exchange notice published before markets opened, Nordwind said routine inspections found cracks in gearbox bearings on several turbines of the same model. As a precaution, the company stopped 14 of the 62 turbines at the 806 MW farm. Inspections of the remaining turbines are ongoing.

The company estimated that the outage would reduce full-year production by around 0.4 TWh and said it would update its financial guidance together with its third-quarter results on 23 October.

Analysts at a Nordic investment bank estimated the direct repair bill at "between EUR 50 and 60 million" in total and said the guidance cut could be in the range of 4–5%. Several analysts noted that the problem appears to be limited to one turbine model and that the company's other wind farms use a different supplier.

"The market is reacting to uncertainty more than to the actual numbers," said one portfolio manager in Oslo. "If the repair schedule holds, this is a two-quarter problem."

Nordwind said safety had not been compromised and that no injuries had occurred. The turbine supplier has confirmed it will provide replacement gearboxes, and the company is in discussions about warranty compensation.
