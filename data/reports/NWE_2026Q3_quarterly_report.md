# Nordwind Energy ASA — Third Quarter 2026 Report

## Highlights

- Revenue of EUR 451 million, up 9% year over year.
- Net income of EUR 29 million, down from EUR 34 million in Q3 2025, mainly due to one-off repair costs at Skarvhavet.
- Installed offshore capacity reached 3.4 GW.
- Full-year 2026 revenue guidance **lowered to EUR 1.72–1.76 billion** (previously EUR 1.80–1.85 billion).

## Operations

Production in the quarter was 2.9 TWh. Fleet availability fell to 91.2% (Q2 2026: 96.8%) after gearbox failures were identified at the Skarvhavet wind farm in early October. As of the date of this report, 14 of the 62 turbines at Skarvhavet remain offline.

The company has secured replacement gearboxes from its supplier and expects all affected turbines to be back in operation by the end of Q1 2027. Repair costs recognised in Q3 2026 amounted to EUR 22 million. Additional costs of up to EUR 35 million are expected in Q4 2026. Part of the lost revenue may be recovered under warranty, but no recovery has been included in the guidance.

The 300 MW Ørnefjell project was commissioned in September, bringing installed capacity to 3.4 GW.

Net margin for the quarter was 6.4%. Net interest-bearing debt increased to EUR 4.1 billion.

## Outlook

Because of the Skarvhavet outage, full-year 2026 revenue guidance is lowered to EUR 1.72–1.76 billion. The Hvalsund project remains on track for a final investment decision in Q1 2027. The Board will propose the annual dividend in February 2027.

CEO Ingrid Halvorsen said: "The Skarvhavet issue is a setback, but it is a technical problem with a clear fix. Our long-term plan is unchanged."
