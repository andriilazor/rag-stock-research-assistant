# Vantera Logistics Corp. — Third Quarter 2026 Results

## Highlights

- Revenue of USD 1.28 billion, up 6% year over year.
- Net income of USD 64 million, a net margin of 5.0% (Q3 2025: USD 55 million, 4.6%).
- Warehousing revenue grew 15%, the fastest-growing segment.
- Agreement signed on 20 October to acquire RailBridge Freight (see separate announcement).

## Segment results

| Segment | Revenue Q3 2026 | Change vs Q3 2025 |
|---|---|---|
| Freight | USD 775m | +2% |
| Warehousing | USD 372m | +15% |
| Last-mile delivery | USD 133m | +5% |

Freight volumes were flat, but pricing improved slightly. Warehousing benefited from two new long-term contracts with e-commerce customers and the opening of a new facility in Indianapolis, bringing the total to 43 warehouses.

The owned fleet grew to approximately 2,900 trucks, including 96 electric trucks used for short-haul routes.

## Costs

Fuel costs fell 4% compared with the previous year. Driver wages increased 5%. Operating ratio improved to 91.8% from 92.6%.

## Balance sheet

Cash at the end of the quarter was USD 310 million. Total debt was USD 1.05 billion. The company repurchased USD 25 million of shares during the quarter.

## Outlook

Vantera raised its full-year 2026 revenue guidance to USD 5.0–5.1 billion (previously USD 4.9–5.0 billion). The guidance does not include the RailBridge acquisition, which is expected to close in Q1 2027.

CEO Marcus Delaney said: "Warehousing continues to be our growth engine, and RailBridge will give us the rail capacity we have been looking for."
