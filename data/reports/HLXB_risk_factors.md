# Helix Biotech Inc. — Summary of Key Risk Factors

*Extract from the 2025 annual report.*

## Dependence on a single product candidate

Our future success depends largely on HX-217. If HX-217 is not approved by regulators, or if approval is significantly delayed, our business and share price would be materially harmed. Our other candidates are at much earlier stages and are not expected to generate revenue for several years.

## Regulatory risk

Even with positive Phase III results, the FDA may request additional data, require a further trial, or approve HX-217 with a narrower label than we expect. Regulatory review in Europe and Japan is subject to separate processes and timelines.

## Manufacturing concentration

We rely on a single contract manufacturer, Lonvia Pharma Services, for the production of HX-217 drug substance. Any disruption at Lonvia's facility in Ireland, such as quality issues, regulatory inspections or capacity limits, could delay clinical supply or a commercial launch. We are qualifying a second manufacturer, but this is not expected to be complete before 2028.

## Royalty concentration

Substantially all of our current revenue comes from royalties on Corvelin, which is sold by our licensing partner. Corvelin's main patent expires in 2029 in the United States. After that date we expect royalty revenue to decline significantly due to generic competition.

## Intellectual property

The composition-of-matter patent for HX-217 expires in 2034 in the United States, with possible extensions of up to five years. Patent challenges by competitors could shorten this period.

## Pricing and reimbursement

Payers increasingly restrict access to high-cost specialty drugs. Even if HX-217 is approved, reimbursement levels may be lower than we assume, which would limit revenue.

## Need for additional capital

We have incurred net losses every year since our founding. If HX-217 is delayed, we may need to raise additional capital, which could dilute existing shareholders.
