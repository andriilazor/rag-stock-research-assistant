# Vantera to buy RailBridge Freight for USD 420 million to expand into rail

**Columbus, Ohio, 20 October 2026** — Vantera Logistics agreed on Tuesday to acquire RailBridge Freight, a Midwest intermodal rail operator, for USD 420 million. Vantera shares rose on the news.

Under the agreement, Vantera will pay USD 290 million in cash and USD 130 million in newly issued Vantera shares. RailBridge operates 11 intermodal terminals and leases about 1,800 containers. It generated revenue of USD 265 million in 2025 and was profitable.

Vantera said the deal would let it move more long-distance freight by rail, where costs per mile are lower and driver shortages are less of an issue. The company expects annual cost savings of about USD 30 million by the end of 2028.

The transaction requires approval from the US Surface Transportation Board and is expected to close in the first quarter of 2027. Vantera plans to fund the cash portion from existing cash and its credit facility.

"This is the missing piece in our network," CEO Marcus Delaney said on a call with analysts. "Customers want one partner for truck, rail and warehousing."

Analysts mostly welcomed the deal. One said the price, about 9 times RailBridge's operating profit, was "reasonable for a strategic asset," while another warned that integrating a rail business would be a new challenge for a company built on trucking.
