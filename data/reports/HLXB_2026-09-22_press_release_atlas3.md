# Helix Biotech announces positive topline results from Phase III ATLAS-3 trial of HX-217

**Cambridge, MA, 22 September 2026** — Helix Biotech Inc. (HLXB) today announced that its Phase III ATLAS-3 trial of HX-217 in patients with idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis (IPF) met its primary endpoint.

ATLAS-3 enrolled 640 patients across 14 countries. Patients received either HX-217 or placebo for 52 weeks. The primary endpoint was the change in forced vital capacity (FVC), a measure of lung function.

## Key results

- Patients treated with HX-217 showed a **31% smaller decline in FVC** compared with placebo (p < 0.001).
- The key secondary endpoint, time to first respiratory hospitalisation, also favoured HX-217 (hazard ratio 0.72).
- The most common side effects were mild to moderate nausea (11% vs 6% with placebo) and diarrhoea (9% vs 5%).
- Discontinuation due to adverse events was 7% in the HX-217 group and 6% in the placebo group.

"These results show that HX-217 can meaningfully slow disease progression in IPF, a condition with very limited treatment options," said Dr. Amara Okafor, Chief Executive Officer of Helix Biotech. "We plan to discuss the data with the FDA and submit a New Drug Application in the first half of 2027."

Full results will be presented at an upcoming medical congress.

## About IPF

Idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis is a chronic lung disease in which lung tissue becomes scarred over time, making breathing increasingly difficult. Existing treatments slow progression only modestly.
