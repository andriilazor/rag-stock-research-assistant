# Vantera Logistics Corp. — Company Overview

Vantera Logistics is a North American freight and supply-chain company headquartered in Columbus, Ohio. It was founded in 2004 as a regional trucking business and listed on the stock exchange in 2015.

## Business segments

| Segment | Share of 2025 revenue | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Freight | 62% | Full-truckload and less-than-truckload transport across the US and Canada |
| Warehousing | 28% | Contract warehousing and fulfilment for retail and e-commerce customers |
| Last-mile delivery | 10% | Home delivery of large items (furniture, appliances) in 30 metropolitan areas |

## Assets

At the end of 2025, Vantera operated a fleet of approximately 2,750 owned trucks and 41 warehouses with a combined area of 1.9 million square metres. The company also uses a network of independent contractors for peak demand.

## Customers

Vantera serves more than 3,000 customers. The ten largest customers account for 27% of revenue. The largest single customer, a national home-improvement retailer, accounts for 8%.

## Strategy

Management's strategy for 2026–2028 focuses on three priorities:

1. Growing the higher-margin warehousing segment.
2. Adding rail and intermodal capacity to reduce dependence on long-haul trucking, where driver shortages and fuel costs pressure margins.
3. Electrifying part of the short-haul fleet, with a target of 400 electric trucks by 2028.

## Leadership

Marcus Delaney has served as Chief Executive Officer since 2019. Priya Raman is Chief Financial Officer.
