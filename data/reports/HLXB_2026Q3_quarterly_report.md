# Helix Biotech Inc. — Third Quarter 2026 Results

## Highlights

- Revenue of USD 96 million, consisting of USD 81 million in royalties from Corvelin (licensed to a partner) and USD 15 million in collaboration revenue.
- Net loss of USD 41 million (Q3 2025: net loss of USD 58 million).
- Positive Phase III results for HX-217 in idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis (ATLAS-3 trial), announced on 22 September.
- Cash, cash equivalents and investments of USD 610 million, expected to fund operations into 2029.

## Pipeline update

**HX-217 (idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis).** The ATLAS-3 Phase III trial met its primary endpoint. Patients treated with HX-217 showed a 31% smaller decline in lung function (forced vital capacity) over 52 weeks compared with placebo. Helix plans to submit a New Drug Application to the U.S. FDA in the first half of 2027.

**HX-305 (systemic sclerosis).** The Phase II trial completed enrolment of 180 patients in August. Topline data are expected in Q3 2027.

**HX-410 (preclinical).** Investigational new drug filing planned for 2027.

## Financial summary

| Item | Q3 2026 | Q3 2025 |
|---|---|---|
| Royalty revenue | USD 81m | USD 74m |
| Collaboration revenue | USD 15m | USD 9m |
| R&D expenses | USD 112m | USD 118m |
| SG&A expenses | USD 31m | USD 27m |
| Net loss | USD 41m | USD 58m |

R&D expenses decreased as the ATLAS-3 trial moved from active treatment to follow-up. SG&A expenses increased as the company began building its commercial organisation ahead of a possible HX-217 launch.

## Outlook

Helix expects full-year 2026 operating expenses of USD 560–580 million. The company does not currently plan to raise additional equity before the FDA decision on HX-217.

CEO Dr. Amara Okafor said: "ATLAS-3 is the most important milestone in Helix's history. Our focus now is a high-quality FDA submission and preparing for launch."

Helix Biotech is headquartered in Cambridge, Massachusetts.
