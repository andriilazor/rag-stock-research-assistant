# Nordwind Energy ASA — Second Quarter 2026 Report

## Highlights

- Revenue of EUR 412 million, up 14% year over year.
- Net income of EUR 38 million (Q2 2025: EUR 31 million).
- Installed offshore capacity reached 3.1 GW after the commissioning of the Lyngvær II extension.
- Full-year 2026 revenue guidance confirmed at **EUR 1.80–1.85 billion**.

## Operations

Nordwind Energy operates offshore wind farms in the North Sea and the Norwegian Sea. In the second quarter, the fleet produced 2.6 TWh of electricity, compared with 2.2 TWh a year earlier. Average availability across the fleet was 96.8%.

The company's largest asset, the Skarvhavet offshore wind farm (62 turbines, 806 MW), delivered availability of 97.4% in the quarter. The Lyngvær II extension added 290 MW and was completed two weeks ahead of schedule.

Average realised power price was EUR 71 per MWh, slightly below the EUR 74 per MWh achieved in Q1 2026 due to strong wind conditions across Northern Europe that pushed down spot prices.

## Financial summary

| Item | Q2 2026 | Q2 2025 |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue | EUR 412m | EUR 361m |
| EBITDA | EUR 196m | EUR 169m |
| Net income | EUR 38m | EUR 31m |
| Capital expenditure | EUR 143m | EUR 210m |

Net interest-bearing debt was EUR 3.9 billion at the end of the quarter. The company paid a dividend of EUR 0.42 per share in May.

## Outlook

Management expects higher production in the second half of the year, as autumn and winter typically bring stronger winds. Full-year 2026 revenue guidance of EUR 1.80–1.85 billion is confirmed. The next major project, Hvalsund (1.2 GW), is expected to reach final investment decision in Q1 2027.

CEO Ingrid Halvorsen commented: "We delivered a strong quarter with record production and an on-time expansion. Our pipeline gives us a clear path to 5 GW by 2030."

Nordwind Energy is headquartered in Bergen, Norway.
